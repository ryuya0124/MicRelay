MicRelay monitors microphone audio through an output on the same iPhone or iPad. No account, server, subscription, or in-app purchase is required.

Monitoring: connect headphones or an HDMI device, allow Microphone, select the built-in or accessory microphone, confirm an external output if requested, and tap Start monitoring. To test without an accessory, tap Start monitoring and explicitly choose the built-in speaker, starting with a low system volume to avoid feedback. Disconnection stops monitoring. Background audio is the core microphone monitoring function; it mixes with other apps. Keep monitoring in background is enabled by default.

Audio settings: Automatic mode verifies settings for the connected equipment. Fully manual mode offers lists of settings, checks whether the requested values were actually applied, and restores the previous setup with an explanation if they were not. The EQ has six bands with optional range-based band-pass and band-stop filters; wind reduction and tap gate have independent switches. The tap gate passes short attacks and can detect sound before or after the enabled filters. Settings can be exported/imported locally as JSON.

Screen recording requires iOS/iPadOS 27 or later. Open Screen recording from the lower settings rows on the main screen, then tap Start screen recording and choose the entire display in the system picker to capture other apps. Exclude monitor audio from recordings is on by default and uses ScreenCaptureKit's excludesCurrentProcessAudio. Turning it off includes MicRelay's output. Direct recording microphone and camera controls are disabled so raw microphone audio is not separately added. Stop recording to change exclusion. Devices below OS 27 show an update notice and disabled recording controls; microphone monitoring still works on iOS 18 or later.

After recording stops, the app requests add-only Photos permission and saves the MP4 to Photos. It removes its own copy only after Photos confirms success. If permission is denied or saving fails, the file remains in the app for retry/export/manual deletion. App storage deletion never deletes assets from Photos. Previously imported app copies are also cleaned up without importing duplicates. No existing photo library is read. Screen-capture background mode supports full-display recording while another app is open. Recording, Photos export, and microphone monitoring are handled locally; no audio, videos, or analytics are sent to the developer.

For microphone testing, stop physical-device screen sharing in Xcode Device Hub: Apple's Device Hub documentation states that it prevents microphone access while interacting with a physical device. The app's own ScreenCaptureKit recording is separate from Device Hub screen sharing.
