Version 1.1.0 improves monitoring, adds more control over tap sounds, and introduces screen recording.

EASIER MONITORING
• Improved automatic tuning to find stable settings with less audio delay for your connected equipment.
• Reduced startup checks and reuse settings already verified for a connection to help monitoring start sooner.
• Reduced waveform display overhead and improved background operation and automatic adjustments.
• If monitoring cannot start, the app now explains why and lets you confirm your output or choose the built-in speaker from the same prompt.

FULLY MANUAL MODE
• Choose audio settings from lists.
• Check whether your requested settings actually work and compare them with the settings in use. Unsupported settings show an explanation and revert to your previous setup.

EQUALIZER AND SOUND ADJUSTMENT
• Reduced frequency graph display overhead to make it easier to compare sound before and after your adjustments.
• Combine the new tap gate with EQ adjustments that reduce a selected range of sounds or let you hear only that range.

TAP GATE
• Pass audio for a short, adjustable time after a sound starts.
• Adjust how long sound passes and how strongly the gate reacts. New attacks can retrigger the gate during rapid repeated taps.
• Combine it with the equalizer and wind noise reduction, and choose whether detection uses sound before or after those adjustments.

SCREEN RECORDING — iOS AND iPadOS 27 OR LATER
• Hear monitor audio through headphones while leaving it out of your video. Exclusion is on by default; turn it off to include monitor audio.
• Recordings are saved to Photos. After saving succeeds, the app automatically removes its own copy.
• If saving fails, keep the video in the app to retry, share, or delete only the app copy. See how much storage the remaining videos use.

SETTINGS BACKUP
• Back up and restore manual audio settings, the tap gate, and recording audio exclusion along with your other settings. Earlier backups remain supported.
