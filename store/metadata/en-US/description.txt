MicRelay lets you hear microphone audio through headphones connected to the same iPhone or iPad.

MICROPHONES AND OUTPUT
Choose the built-in microphone or a headset or external microphone made available by the OS. Wired, USB, and Bluetooth audio outputs are supported. Monitoring cannot start without headphones and stops when they are disconnected. The built-in speaker is available only when you explicitly enable it.

GAIN AND EQUALIZER
Set monitor gain and choose the gain slider’s maximum. Your gain settings are kept after restarting the app.
The six-band equalizer lets you adjust each band’s frequency, gain, and Q. Notch, low-cut, and high-cut filters are also available. Compare the live input and post-EQ frequency spectra while adjusting the filters. Turning EQ off bypasses filter processing.

MONITORING AND DISPLAY
Audio mode and I/O buffer settings adapt to your device and connection. You can also choose Automatic, Standard, or Measurement mode manually. View the input waveform, input level, and input/output latency reported by the OS. With background monitoring enabled, audio continues while the screen is locked or another app is open.
The layout adapts to the available screen width on iPad and iPhone Duo.

SETTINGS BACKUP
Export settings to a JSON file and restore them using the Files app. Review the imported settings before restoring gain, gain limit, EQ, audio mode, and other preferences together.

LIMITATIONS
Actual latency depends on your device, OS, and connected hardware. Bluetooth adds wireless transmission and codec latency. Some microphone and output combinations cannot be selected together by the OS. Exclusive microphone use by another app and system interruptions can stop monitoring.
Using the speaker can cause feedback. Amplification beyond audio full scale is clipped.

Audio is processed on your device. It is not recorded or sent to a server. No account is required. Source code and license notices are available from a dedicated screen in the app.

Available in Japanese and English.
