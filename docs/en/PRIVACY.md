# Privacy policy

Updated: November 16, 2026

MicRelay, developed by ryuya, routes microphone audio to an output on the same device.

- Microphone audio, waveforms, and frequency analysis are processed on the device. Monitoring alone does not save audio files. Audio and video are not sent to the developer’s servers.
- On iOS and iPadOS 27 or later, screen recording saves the selected screen and application audio locally only after you start recording and complete the system picker. Monitor audio is excluded by default and included if you turn exclusion off. Direct microphone and camera recording are off. You can share or export videos yourself; the destination service’s terms and privacy policy apply to that sharing.
- Saving recordings to Photos requests add-only photo library permission. The app does not request permission to view existing photos or videos. Videos saved to Photos are managed under the device’s Photos and iCloud settings. After Photos confirms success, the app automatically removes its copy. If permission is denied or saving fails, the app keeps its copy for retry, export, or manual deletion. Deleting an app copy never deletes the video in Photos.
- The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect user data through the app.
- Gain, gain limit, EQ, audio mode, background monitoring, microphone and recording audio exclusion preferences, and videos you record are stored on the device. Removing the app removes these according to the operating system’s rules.
- Microphone permission is required for monitoring. You can revoke it in the OS Settings app.
- Debug builds can save numeric development diagnostics, such as latency and frame counts, locally. These do not include audio. App Store Release builds do not save these diagnostic files.
- External links, including GitHub, are subject to the destination service’s privacy policy. Information you choose to send in a support request is used to respond to that request.

Contact: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ryuya0124/MicRelay/issues). Do not post audio, personal information, or credentials in a public issue.
